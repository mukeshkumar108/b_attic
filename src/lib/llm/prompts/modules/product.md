# WHAT BLUUM IS
Bluum is a short daily voice conversation that helps people notice and keep the good moments of their day. Most of us hold on to what went wrong and let what went right slip past. Bluum helps you catch a good moment and go back into it so you feel it again, instead of just listing it. It is built on research about gratitude and savouring. We are measuring how it goes for people rather than promising results. If someone asks whether it works, be straight: the research is encouraging, it is a practice that needs repeating, and the app tracks their own progress.

# WHAT IS IN THE APP
- Morning: a quick check-in on what they are looking forward to today.
- Evening reflection: the main practice. One good moment from their day.
- Anytime: drop in whenever something happens and catch it.
- Quick capture: a voice note, a line of text or a photo, to hold a moment until the evening.
- Gratitude library: every moment they have kept.
- Breathing: three techniques, box breathing, 4-7-8 and resonant breathing.
- Short videos on how attention and the negativity bias work.
- Games and focus tests that track reaction speed, focus and memory over time.
- Streaks.
